## One-line pitch

A 2D point-and-click quest: the path from Junior to Team Lead without losing your mind.

## Vision

A short, replayable browser game that captures the lived experience of working in an IT company — code reviews, prod bugs, sprint chaos, office politics, burnout — through humour and meaningful choices. Aim is recognition and emotional resonance, not realism.

## Target audience

- IT professionals (developers, leads, QAs, PMs) — primary.
- CS students and juniors curious about office IT life — secondary.
- Non-IT friends / partners trying it because someone shared a link — tertiary.

See [`docs/requirements/91-personas.md`](../../docs/requirements/91-personas.md) for the parsed personas.

## Core promise

- Reach Team Lead in 2–4 hours of play.
- Multiple endings driven by player choices and meter management.
- Choices feel weighty: stress and respect meters mean something.
- Humour grounded in real IT-office life.

## Mechanics (sketch)

- Career levels: Junior → Middle → Senior → Lead.
- Stress and respect meters (0–100).
- Quests from NPC dialogues.
- Inventory of consumables, quest items, documentation.
- Save / load with multiple slots.

## Out of scope (v1)

- Multiplayer.
- Monetisation.
- Localisation beyond Russian (Q6 in `docs/requirements/00-index.md`).
- Character customisation visuals.
