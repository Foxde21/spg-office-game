# Customers Skill Matrix – Product Management

## Skills Matrix

### Department: Product Management

## Product thinking & strategy
- Junior: understands product goals and basic prioritization; supports discovery activities
- Middle: defines outcomes, tracks metrics, and maintains a roadmap for an area
- Senior: owns product strategy for a product/stream; aligns stakeholders and drives trade-offs
- Expert: sets product vision and portfolio strategy; builds product operating model

## Customer discovery & research
- Junior: participates in interviews; documents insights under supervision
- Middle: plans and runs discovery; synthesizes insights into problem statements
- Senior: drives continuous discovery; validates assumptions with experiments
- Expert: defines discovery standards; mentors teams; scales discovery across org

## Requirements & communication
- Junior: writes clear user stories and acceptance criteria with guidance
- Middle: independently writes requirements; facilitates refinement sessions
- Senior: drives alignment across teams; manages scope and ambiguity
- Expert: creates system-level narratives; improves communication processes

## Prioritization & roadmap
- Junior: uses simple prioritization techniques; supports backlog grooming
- Middle: prioritizes based on value, risk, and effort; manages dependencies
- Senior: balances short-term delivery with long-term investments; handles conflicts
- Expert: sets portfolio priorities; drives governance and decision-making

## Delivery & execution
- Junior: supports sprint execution and release coordination
- Middle: ensures delivery predictability; works closely with design/engineering
- Senior: leads cross-functional execution; mitigates risks; improves delivery processes
- Expert: defines delivery standards; drives org-wide execution excellence

## Metrics & analytics
- Junior: tracks basic metrics; understands funnels and KPIs at a high level
- Middle: defines success metrics; uses analytics to drive decisions
- Senior: builds metric systems; connects metrics to strategy and experiments
- Expert: defines measurement culture; drives data-informed product org

## Stakeholder & customer relations
- Junior: participates in customer/stakeholder communication
- Middle: independently runs stakeholder syncs; manages expectations
- Senior: builds trust with key stakeholders; leads difficult conversations
- Expert: manages strategic customer relationships; influences executives

## Quality & ownership
- Junior: responsible for quality of product artifacts and basic acceptance
- Middle: ensures quality of releases and acceptance criteria
- Senior: sets quality bar; drives improvements to product quality processes
- Expert: responsible for quality standards across product portfolio
