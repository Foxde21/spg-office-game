
# Business Analysis Skill Matrix

Структурированная версия матрицы уровней и требований.

## Уровни
- **Junior**
- **Middle**
- **Senior**
- **Expert**

---

## 1. Professional Experience
### Junior
- Good sense to understand and analyze business processes.
- Familiar with SPG trainee program.
- 1+ year of relevant experience.

### Middle
- 3+ years of relevant experience incl. UML.
- Follows SDLC with minimal supervision.

### Senior
- 5+ years experience including UML, Modeling, Design.
- Expert knowledge in at least 1 domain.
- Mentoring ability.

### Expert
- 7+ years relevant experience.
- Expert in SPG domains.
- Able to supervise and develop other BAs.

---

## 2. Software Process Knowledge
### Junior
- No production experience required.
- Works under supervision.

### Middle
- Strong SDLC knowledge.
- Agile/Waterfall.

### Senior
- Expert in BA and SDLC processes.
- Risk, scope, estimation.

### Expert
- Expert in industry standards.
- Agile evangelist.

---

## 3. Design & Documentation Skills
### Junior
- Reads documentation.
- Creates documentation under supervision.

### Middle
- Creates analysis/requirements independently.
- Uses visualization tools.

### Senior
- High-quality documentation.
- Process optimization.

### Expert
- Excellent documentation.
- Can combine analytics & visualization deeply.

---

## 4. Business Domain Knowledge
### Junior
- Not required.

### Middle
- Basic domain understanding.
- Assists SMEs.

### Senior
- Good domain knowledge.
- Can deputize SMEs.

### Expert
- Deep domain expertise.
- Teaches business concepts.

---

## 5. Roles on a Project
### Junior
- Plays BA/SA role.

### Middle
- Can be Scrum Master.
- Performs cross-reviews.

### Senior
- Key BA.
- Can be SM, PO, PM.

### Expert
- Team Lead, Reviewer, Auditor.
- PO, PM.

---

## 6. Reliability
### Junior
- Works under supervision.

### Middle
- Works without supervision.
- Mentors Juniors.

### Senior
- Self-managing.
- Interviews candidates.

### Expert
- Full responsibility for subsystem.
- Single point of contact.

---

## 7. Quality
### Junior
- Responsible for quality of own deliverables.

### Middle
- Good quality outputs.
- Full responsibility for results.

### Senior
- High quality.
- Responsible for team deliverables.

### Expert
- Excellent quality.
- Defines/improves quality processes.

---

## 8. Customer Relations
### Junior
- Participates in calls.
- Collects data with supervision.

### Middle
- Facilitates workshops.
- Builds relationships.

### Senior
- Works with top-tier clients.
- Organizes visits.

### Expert
- Builds long-term relationships.
- Handles strategic communication.

---

## 9. English & Customer Language
### Junior → Expert
- B1 → B2+.
- Expert fully fluent.

---

## 10. Responsibilities
### Junior
- BA activities on Delivery Horizon.

### Middle
- Requirements engineering on Initiative Horizon.

### Senior
- Strategy Horizon.
- Discovery & solution vision.

### Expert
- Presale.
- BA process setup.
- Defines KPIs.

---

### Middle

### Senior

- UX techniques.

### Expert
- Chooses techniques.
- Cybersecurity & Data analysis awareness.
